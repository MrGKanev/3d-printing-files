## Notes
- Tablet dimensions 211 x 124.7 x 8 mm (spec sheet). Measure yours (case?) and adjust the parameters in model.py.
- The USB-C port position is ASSUMED at the center of the short side - verify it and tune port_off.
- The top edge is fully open and the tablet sticks out 30 mm (grab), so the power/volume buttons stay free. Raise grab for more room.
- A right-angle USB-C plug is recommended. Mount with 4 screws (~4 mm) through the countersunk holes.
